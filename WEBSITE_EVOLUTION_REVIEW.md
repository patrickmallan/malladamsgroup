# Website evolution review

The revised site presents Malladams Group as the company, Energetic Architecture as its foundational practice, Annissa's private organizational leadership engagements as the current offering, and future environments as a longer-term vision.

| Earlier site section | Decision | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero and visual identity | Refine | Retained the forest, cream, gold, serif and spacious layout. Replaced the broad claim about structures with the new philosophy and a clear route to learn more. |
| The Gap and The Problem | Refine | Kept “Something is missing from the blueprint,” shortened the copy, and removed unsupported universal claims and promised outcomes. |
| Three-layer framework | Move | Land, physical space and operating structures now live under Our Vision, where they describe future work. |
| Values | Keep and refine | All four values remain on the Energetic Architecture page as questions rather than guaranteed effects. |
| What We Do | Remove and replace | Removed claims that Malladams Group acquires and develops properties now. Added the current leadership offering and a separate future vision. |
| Our First Project | Refine and move | Renamed to “Where our vision begins.” Squamish is described as a meaningful connection, not a development underway. |
| Founders | Refine | Kept a warm family introduction and image. Distinguished Annissa's current work from Patrick's contribution to future experiences and spaces. |
| Contact | Refine | Shortened the form, added area of interest, removed the claim that both founders lead every engagement, and asks visitors to omit confidential personnel details. |
